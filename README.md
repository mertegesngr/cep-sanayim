# Cep Sanayim 🚗

### Your Automotive Marketplace & Service Platform

**Cep Sanayim** is a mobile application project designed to bring automotive spare parts, professional mechanics, and vehicle maintenance services together in one digital platform.

The project is currently under development, initially targeting the Turkish automotive market.

## About the Project

Cep Sanayim aims to simplify vehicle ownership by providing an integrated marketplace where vehicle owners can find compatible spare parts, discover automotive professionals, and schedule maintenance or repair appointments.

The platform combines e-commerce, service discovery, and appointment booking in a single mobile application.

## Planned Features

- **Spare Parts Marketplace:** Browse, compare, and purchase automotive spare parts.
- **Mechanic Discovery:** Find automotive mechanics and repair shops.
- **Appointment Booking:** Schedule vehicle maintenance and repair services.
- **VIN Decoding:** Identify vehicles using their Vehicle Identification Number.
- **Vehicle Profiles:** Save vehicle information for personalized services.
- **Parts Compatibility:** Help users identify spare parts compatible with their vehicles.
- **Service Comparison:** Explore and compare automotive service providers.

## VIN Decoding Integration

VIN decoding is an important component of the platform.

Users will be able to enter or scan their vehicle's VIN to retrieve information such as:

- Vehicle manufacturer and brand
- Model and production year
- Engine specifications
- Fuel type
- Vehicle variant

This information will support vehicle identification, spare parts filtering, and service recommendations.

## Target Market

**Initial Market:** Türkiye

The application aims to support vehicles from European, Asian, and other international automotive manufacturers.

## Development Status

**Status:** Early Development / Prototype

Cep Sanayim is currently an independent software engineering project. Features and integrations described above are planned and subject to development and testing.

## Project Vision

To create an accessible automotive ecosystem that connects vehicle owners, spare parts suppliers, and service professionals through one convenient mobile platform.
